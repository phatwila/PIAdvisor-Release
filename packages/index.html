What is new in v1.5.0

A rebuilt chat, live image measurements, and easier image sharing on Windows and Linux.

  • Rebuilt the chat and message input: full-width messages, matching PixInsight light and dark themes, clearer message controls, and one-click Markdown copying. New Chat shows a welcome guide with provider setup and formatting examples.
  • Long conversations load older messages as you scroll and keep your reading position.
  • Live image measurements. With "Let the advisor fetch image data" on, PIAdvisor can measure your open images during a reply: star FWHM and eccentricity, background noise and gradients, statistics, stretch predictions, history entries, process parameters and FITS keywords. Star measurements use PixInsight's built-in star detection and PSF fitting. Measurements run in the background and never change your images.
  • A live Checking line shows what PIAdvisor is measuring. Show measured values opens the exact results, and follow-up questions keep key measurements until the image changes. In each measuring round, PIAdvisor can take several measurements at once. Free allows 2 rounds per reply. Pro and Trial allow up to 12.
  • Exact settings come from your installation: before PIAdvisor suggests settings for a process, it can read that process's parameter names, ranges and options from your PixInsight.
  • Paste PNG or JPEG screenshots directly into the chat input with Ctrl+V on Windows and Linux. Images appear in the draft attachment ribbon without sending or replacing your text. You can also send an image without a written message.
  • Click an attached image to open a separate, resizable viewer. Click to zoom around the pointer, drag to pan, then click again to fit the whole image. Originals stay local at their imported or captured resolution; the model receives resized images. Imports run in the background.
  • Each reply shows how long it took, including measuring.
  • A line under each reply shows token usage, cached tokens and request count; OpenRouter also reports cost. Conversation requests reuse provider prompt caching where supported to reduce repeated processing and cost.
  • About my setup in LLM Settings saves your gear and preferences, such as processes you avoid, and includes them with every request.
  • Updated advisor guidance names problems first and backs findings with measurements.
  • If you changed the system prompt or the formatting template, PIAdvisor offers the new prompts at startup and saves a copy of yours first.
  • Better provider compatibility and clearer error messages.
  • Fixes for measuring, message text, history-preview privacy and license activation.
